Sample Brand Knowledge Document

Brand: TechCorp
Industry: Technology Solutions

Brand Voice:
Our brand voice is innovative, forward-thinking, and accessible. We speak with authority but remain approachable and human.

Visual Identity:
- Primary colors: Deep blue (#003366) and bright cyan (#00BFFF)
- Typography: Modern sans-serif fonts
- Imagery: Clean, high-tech visuals with people using technology in real-world scenarios

Target Audience:
- Business decision-makers aged 35-55
- IT professionals and technology enthusiasts
- Companies seeking digital transformation

Key Messages:
- Innovation that drives business forward
- Technology made simple and accessible
- Trusted partner for digital transformation
- Human-centered technology solutions

Brand Values:
- Innovation
- Reliability
- Customer success
- Continuous improvement

Tone Guidelines:
- Professional but not stuffy
- Confident but not arrogant
- Technical but not jargon-heavy
- Helpful and solution-oriented
